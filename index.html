Five steps, in order

  1. Explore the training methods you can realistically access.
  2. Pick one main goal and, if useful, one or two secondary goals.
  3. Understand your current physical starting point, by what you can do rather than your body type.
  4. Notice the activities you enjoy, your schedule and what you need to recover.
  5. Set a preliminary direction and note what needs a closer look.

The result is a starting direction, not a finished program. Repeat the workbook after a few weeks of consistent training to see what changed.
